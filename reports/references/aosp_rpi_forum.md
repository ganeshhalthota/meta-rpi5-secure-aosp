## Overview

Development thread for custom Android (AOSP) builds for Raspberry Pi hardware, maintained by
"Konsta". Tracks Android 15 QPR2 and upcoming Android 16 ("Baklava") for RPi4 and RPi5.

## Supported Devices

| Device | Minimum RAM |
|--------|------------|
| Pi 4 (Model B, Pi 400, Compute Module 4) | 2 GB |
| Pi 5 (including Pi 500 and Compute Module 5) | — |

## Latest Build (as of thread)

- Android 15 QPR2 (March 6, 2025)
- Linux kernel 6.12 option available
- HWC3 and AIDL HAL implementations
- Enhanced display and audio DAC support

## Known Issues / Discussion Points

- Camera recording limitations with HQ camera module
- Temperature management: fan configuration options
- Audio gain adjustment
